# Ann Lite

**Priye. Aprann. Espere. Sèvi.**
*Pray. Learn. Hope. Serve.*

A Christian platform for prayer, Bible reading, reflection, and
transparent charitable giving — in Haitian Creole, French, and English.

Founded by **Berline Britus**.

</div>

---

## What we're building

Ann Lite helps people pray, read Scripture, read Christian reflections,
grow spiritually, and give transparently to support vulnerable children,
orphans, and vulnerable elders — with card and Celo (CELO/cUSD)
donations, and public on-chain verification for crypto gifts.

We never promise salvation or a place in heaven in exchange for using
the platform or donating. See
[`ann-lite-docs/docs/PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md`](https://github.com/ann-lite/ann-lite-docs/blob/main/docs/PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md)
for our full ethical principles.

## Status

**Pre-production.** Real, tested architecture across all six product
repositories; not yet connected to a live payment provider or a
production Celo deployment, and the smart contract has not yet been
professionally audited. See
[`ann-lite-docs/docs/PRODUCTION_CHECKLIST.md`](https://github.com/ann-lite/ann-lite-docs/blob/main/docs/PRODUCTION_CHECKLIST.md)
for exact current status.
